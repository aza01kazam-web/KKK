Chapter II · Confession

Something I Wanted To Tell You

Words carried quietly for two years, finally set free.

The letter I wrote
Graduation, 2026
To the one who made ordinary days lighter,

I've liked you since first year — though "had a crush on you" is the more honest way to put it. For a long time I chose to keep that quietly to myself. Most of what I felt stayed quiet, but it stayed real. Even from a distance, your presence gave me a kind of inspiration that made ordinary days feel lighter.

There was even a time when napunta yung usapan namin sa mga crush sa school, and to go along with the conversation, I ended up mentioning mine — and yes, it was you. Somehow the story spread, madaldal kasi tong isa kong kaibigan na naka hikari sunscreen na parang bading HAHAHA hanggang sa ayun, umabot kina Jash at Sir Kim HAHAHA.

Hindi ako yung tipong tao na nagagawa nang ganto — I genuinely did not know how to say this. It took me a long time to find the right words and the right moment, not because I was afraid, but because they mattered enough that I wanted to say them with honesty and respect.

Now that I am gradated, I felt I should finally be honest.. I want to thank you for being someone who made a positive impact on my life, even if we were not close. Your presence, even from afar, was a source of inspiration and comfort during times when I needed it most.

I also want to thank you for the small kindnesses you may not have realized I would remember. Being accepted on Facebook, talking on Messenger, sharing even brief conversations — those became moments I treasured more than I ever showed.

At first, I thought it was just a simple crush. Pero habang mas nakikilala kita, unti-unting nag-iba yung nararamdaman ko. The more I learned about you, the more I found myself wanting to genuinely pursue you — not just admire you from afar.

Siguro ang simple pakinggan nung lagi mong sinasabi na “bahay at kapilya lang” ginagawa mo, pero honestly, that was one of the things that made me admire you even more. There was something so sincere and comforting about the way you devoted your time to your church duties and carried yourself with quiet kindness.

In a world where a lot of people try so hard to impress others, you stood out to me because you felt genuine. And maybe that’s when I realized it stopped being just a crush. I slowly started falling for the person you are, not just the person I see.

I am saying this now not to disturb your peace or ask for anything in return. I simply did not want to leave without telling you that you became one of the most beautiful parts of this period of my life. It's just that I want to free myself from the quiet weight of having never said it.

So yes, I have a crush on you, and I am glad I finally found the courage to say it properly. This letter is not a request. It is simply my way of honoring what I felt, and of thanking you for being someone I will always remember with warmth.

— Paolo
